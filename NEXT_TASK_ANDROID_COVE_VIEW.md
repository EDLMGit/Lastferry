## Two instruments the player can read at a glance

Keep the objective recognizable with the lighthouse and home count. Replace the persistent load and depth sentences with two compact, original ferry instruments. Use deck slots that fill per occupied **space**, including passengers who take two or three. For depth, enlarge the existing ticked gauge enough to read its water fill and separate required-draft marker. Match the existing teal, sea glass and lantern gold. Shape and position must carry the distinction with color removed. Small numerals are welcome where they help. Preserve a comfortable tap target even as the visual footprint shrinks.

In the reference state, Pip, Rue and Moss fill all four deck spaces, the hull needs depth 3, local water is 5 and zero of three are home. A free tap should give an exact plain-language reading and passenger names. Keep equivalent accessible names and keyboard/TalkBack access. Keep actions taken and the next relevant flood location/countdown visible in the separate tide instrument. Keep the reason for blocked boarding at the point of choice. Opening either instrument is free. Check the same Rush Landing berth at water 3. Boarding Rowan with Button needs depth 3 and works. With Thistle, it needs 4 and is blocked. Undo, passenger selection and tide changes must immediately update the readings.
