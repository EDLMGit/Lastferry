# The cloth comes back

The two visits to the refuge share a stone quay and a russet-and-ivory checked cloth. On the first visit Tansy carries the raised hem along the table, lets it fall and looks up. Moss settles to her left. Bram waits beside the brass bell. A much smaller occupied landing answers across the water. On the final visit the same cloth carries a meal, Orin’s fruiting bough and a cup and plate beside an empty slatted chair. Nell’s rope leads from the mooring to the yellow ferry. The two rabbits, Mallow and Osier are already safely on the quay.

The supper has one pass of warm light. Its bearing moves from the distant water over the gathering, with soft edges and distance falloff. The beam fades away, leaving the lantern burning. The last 1.3 seconds of the eight-second illustration are still. The player can linger there indefinitely, replay from More, or choose Continue to credits. ## What changed in the pictures

In the first phone compositions the distant landing appeared above the water and hills. At supper the chair legs and one table leg extended beyond the top face of the quay. I kept the original watercolor palette, adjusted the horizon to the far bank and gave the gathering enough stone surface to support the furniture and mooring. The lamp now has a visible water reflection. The raised cloth follows Tansy’s paw before she releases it. [First isolated table](art-first/last-lights-374-7.png) · [First isolated supper](art-first/supper-374-8.png) · [Original actual phone scene](play/first-pass/last-lights-390x844.png)

In the return to the orchard, the fruit tree and three stepped stones recall the earlier encounter. Damson’s feet and Brindle stand on the dry landing above the empty deck. I separated the ferry’s cabin from Damson’s legs after they touched in the first landscape painting. At Far Shore I also moved Button away from Thistle’s face so both read at the first dock. The Last Ferry illustration leaves its four passengers on their two respective shores and the boat in open water.
