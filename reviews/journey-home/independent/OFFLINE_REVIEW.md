# The Long Tide portable Journey

Open `Last-Ferry-The-Long-Tide.html` in a browser. A fresh captain starts at **Home → Play** and can follow the twelve-cove Journey through the lighthouse supper and credits. The download supplies the game. Journey and mastery progress belong to the browser’s local storage. Someone opening a copy can start their own voyage.
