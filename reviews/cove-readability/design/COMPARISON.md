# Designing the two readings

The deciding landscape moment was the first sight of Harbor. With the measurements laid out in a single upper strip, the sign was 433 pixels wide and cut across the lantern and roof. The 154-pixel side instrument left the full lighthouse visible, with space to see the boat, Moss and both rabbits. On the upright screen, stacking the same readings made the sign 132 pixels tall. The retained portrait arrangement puts the destination and rescue count above the load and water readings and takes 79 pixels of height. [Landscape strip](780x320-strip.png) · [Final side instrument](780x320-fitted.png) · [Tall portrait](390x844-strip.png) · [Final compact portrait](390x844-fitted.png)

The first side instrument said “To Harbor.” At the first dock that can read as a sailing instruction. The retained heading says “Bring all 3 to Harbor” above the current count. Beneath it the sign shows who is aboard, how much space they occupy, the depth they need and the water available here. The lighthouse glyph also appears beside Harbor on the map. Selecting the sign opens the crossing details with a free Find Harbor action. [First layout](780x320-side.png)

The forecast went through a separate fit check. Pairing its wave with elapsed actions made the 0 look connected to the 4 at Rabbit Pier. Moving the glyph to the imminent change fixed that reading, but widening the corner sign to 251 pixels made the centre of Shallows hit the forecast control. Constraining the same text to 219 pixels restored the marker but wrapped the two counts and covered the Rabbit Pier label. The retained 212 × 73-pixel sign groups the brass wave with “In 4 actions”, gives “0 actions used” its own divider and leaves the pier name and waiting pair legible on the map. [Intercepted Shallows](780x320-retained.png) · [Wrapped counts](780x320-joined.png) · [Restored map and controls](780x320-fitted.png) · [Measured hit owners](fitted-measurements.json)

## Wider coves and rotation

Wide Berth exposed a problem at 780 × 320 that Holding could not show. The reviewed build left Sorrel and Finch’s faces visible above its forecast at y266. The larger forecast started at y222 and hid both. The ferry reading also covered the Harbor lantern. All three map targets stayed in the same positions across the comparison. The first frame comes from the actual minified reviewed build `7edba1519e9d856f3dc029bb0d500b4480985149`. The middle frame comes from the working development build before the correction. [Reviewed Wide Berth](wide-780-baseline.png) · [First larger signs](wide-780-retained.png) · [Initial measurements](wide-play-observations.json)

Short-landscape instruments now keep their ordinary corners where they fit. At a settled frame with a conflict, the sign uses the closest clear position along the same edge, with the adjoining edge available when needed. It accounts for the projected animals, ferry, lighthouse, stop targets and other controls. The camera keeps its authored scale. At Wide Berth the ferry reading moves to x111 and the forecast to x380. The lantern and both rabbits are visible. In Driftwood Whole cove the forecast leaves Fern, Oat and Reed Landing visible on the right. At Far Shore the forecast clears the Rush Landing target. An earlier trigger moved Holding’s sign over lower water for a two-pixel contact with a target’s margin. Testing the usable interior of the target kept Holding’s preferred corner. [Wide result](wide-berth-780-edge.png) · [Driftwood Whole cove](driftwood-yard-780-edge.png) · [Far Shore](far-shore-780-edge.png) · [Earlier over-sensitive Holding placement](holding-pier-780-edge.png)

The first development regression run passed four cases. The fifth found Fern and Oat underneath Driftwood’s Whole-cove forecast. An early landscape adjustment also failed a five-point touch check at Rush Landing after rotation. Keeping the positions tied to the settled camera revision corrected that case. The five existing regressions then passed, covering place-index focus, actual fullscreen entry/exit, two-finger transfer safety, free inspection and five-point targets at five viewport sizes, and five Whole-cove passenger/lighthouse clearance comparisons. [Original four-pass/one-failure report](regressions-first.json) · [Five passing cases](regressions.json) · [Original Wide Play failure](layout-first.json)

The last check touched open water in Wide’s 780 × 320 Play view with the forecast shifted 173 pixels left, panned and rotated to 390 × 844. Both signs returned to x8, stayed inside the visible portrait, and a tap opened the forecast at action zero. Seven further actual Play observations across Holding, Wide and Far passed, checking all five hit points of every fully visible target, the visible passenger upper centres, both controls’ fit and free Find Harbor. Holding also covered 320 × 740; in 780 × 320 all four stop targets retained all five points. The checks used Chromium 156.0.8078.4 with SwiftShader and DPR 1. The panned rotation ran on the final source after the five legacy regressions.
