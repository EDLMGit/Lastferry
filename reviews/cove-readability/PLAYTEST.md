# Fresh-player retest

Give the player the root Journey with an empty local checkpoint and the default settings. Let them choose Play and take the helm. At first control, ask one question.

> What are you trying to do, and what do you expect to change first?

Let the player act or inspect freely. Record their first answer verbatim, the first place or control they use, what they expect before committing, and what they think happened after the first rise. If they ask for help, note the question and when they asked it. Keep this page with the observer.
