# Original phone preflight

The first scratch production build exposed a composition defect at 390×844. Signal Quay and the ferry were visible at the left, with Wick Landing on the right. The span extended off the left edge, including during operation from the quay. The same bridge, ferry and Wick Landing fit in 780×320 landscape. `portrait-before.png` preserves the actual initial Play view. Its JavaScript SHA-256 is `8ddf4390dac439e76ee5f1f10a8245a852cbd46873fb4ae874c4f53171bf71f5` and its CSS SHA-256 is `57c4d4113b47aa32a9972bb05f9dba1c3b8d2a6b8b9eb9f6d77eca8d1984e936`. The scratch bundle was built over base `e583ec29578e7561c0a8ab8fe6e3b08275d44ac2` during implementation.

The first scratch preview process exited with its launching shell. The initial browser navigation received `ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED`. Running Vite as a persistent process restored the preview. The first portrait UI run reached and opened the bridge. The harness then tried to tap Harbor as the camera finished returning from Whole cove. Harbor moved outside Play view. The harness now uses the real Whole cove control if the target moves before the tap.

The short-landscape preflight played the early-opening loss, undid both actions, rescued everyone by the detour, undid delivery and brought everyone home again. Its source check failed because implementation files changed between the beginning and end of the run. The original result is in `landscape-source-check.json`. The release checks and evidence in the sibling `gameplay` folder use the final bundle.

An intermediate camera-corrected build passed the full portrait shortcut check. The geometry review then found a mid-swing conflict with the stationary ferry. The landscape UI reached the end of its rescue and redelivery, but its end-of-run source digest changed while the pivot was corrected. `before-sweep-source-check.json` retains both intermediate results. The entire phone pair and its recording were repeated on the corrected pivot.
