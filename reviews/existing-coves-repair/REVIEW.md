# Wide Berth and Reef Return

[Wide Berth](http://127.0.0.1:5270/?cove=wide-berth) · [Reef Return](http://127.0.0.1:5270/?cove=reef-return)

The existing six-space ferry has a deeper yellow hull and a red wheelhouse. The narrow shore landings meet a side gate. The two repaired coves ask different questions about the way home. They retain their existing IDs, Journey roles and saved completion history.

- **Wide Berth.** Beacon Reef runs homeward, outward and finally homeward. The first homeward passage is too shallow for more than two occupied spaces. There are eight spaces of neighbors to rescue. A dry High Quay helps with staging, but it cannot give the ferry another return trip. Outer Buoy connects the pickup shores. [Production proof and two routes](WIDE_DESIGN.md)
- **Reef Return.** The deep reef and the shallow approach through Shell Pool lead home under different loads. The ferry can gather a full boat for the reef, or deliver a smaller group first and come back light through the shoal. The later floods remove the chance to put someone ashore and change draft. [Production proof and two routes](REEF_DESIGN.md)
