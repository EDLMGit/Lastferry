# Water-motion references and adaptation

Read on2026-10-24; implementation uses installed Three0.186.1. These explain techniques, not device-performance guarantees.

- [Three — How to update Things](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/how-to-update-things.html): preallocate attributes and change active draw range rather than resize GPU buffers. FerryWake reuses one mesh, six attribute arrays and a fixed96-point ring; no point/vector/geometry allocation is needed for emission or joins.
- [Mark Finch /Cyan Worlds — Effective Water Simulation from Physical Models](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-1-effective-water-simulation-physical-models): analytic surface orientation informed the localized normal trial. Last Ferry tested two bounded Gaussian hull/bow slope lobes in its existing shader, then rejected them after native phone on/off comparison did not demonstrate a consistent gain. The accepted sea shader remains unchanged; no full simulation or rendering pipeline was imported.
- [Unity Technical Content Team — HDRP water system](https://discussions.unity.com/t/the-hdrp-water-system-in-unity-2022-lts/359727): conceptual distinction between deformation and foam/contact informed the experiment. The final treatment uses local bow/stern crests plus a historical trail, with no Unity package or claimed physical model.
- [MDN — WebGL best practices](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/WebGL_API/WebGL_best_practices): bounded resources, batching and proportionate fragment work. Trail and contacts share one draw and reuse the existing coastal mask. No extra texture, target or global water sampling pass is added. Transparent overdraw still exists; one draw alone is not proof of low GPU cost.

World-space samples are emitted by horizontal travelled distance, with interpolated birth time/center/heading. Smooth joined side vectors stay bounded; segment IDs prevent a strip joining different moves. The stored history stays put horizontally, while drawn vertices track the animated water height. Actual hull heading places contacts; displacement governs strength. Normal docking owns only a finite650ms contact release and1800ms history expiry. Recovery and discontinuity use clearing rather than natural docking.
