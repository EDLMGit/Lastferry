# Drowned Orchard: the usable shore has moved

Harbor and its lighthouse are still west, the apple tree and landing sit east, Cider Quay occupies the middle, and Spring Steps lie to the south. The island footprints, berth and shore anchor coordinates, Orchard camera orientation and original landmark anchors match C7. An additional, smaller instance of the existing fruit tree stands on the low quay beside the cider crate. Its lower trunk and the crate disappear into the rising water. The high orchard tree remains on dry ground. No alternate water shader, hull, animal mesh, UI portrait or camera mode was added.

The old Quay–Steps passage is gone. Spring Steps now connects directly around the eastern shore to Orchard Landing. The curved approach brings the ferry away from the pier before turning; it does not cut across the steps. The four candidate landing profiles are 1.62 world units wide (C7: 1.47–1.52). This gives the real beaver feet supported space beside the transfer aisle without tightening the spacing between a moving animal and the waiting group. The candidate shares Ternhaven's proven deer/sheep/beaver/rabbit deck and shore seat pattern and side-gate transfer alignment. All original level data, seat coordinates and animal scales remain untouched.

## Checks, original failures and qualifications

- [Final physical checks](geometry-final.json): **16/16 selected tests passed**. Actual transformed hull envelope vs island contours, every pier mesh and current rock; bidirectional motion paths sampled at 1,001 positions per heading. Real model body/foot bounds, all 14 capacity-legal rosters including empty, all four neighbors ashore, both berth orientations, all load/draft heights, nonempty supported feet, gate/shore/deck walks and mutually legal transfer subsets. All three reachable tides were used; a flooded pier has no legal transfer frames. Existing model/clearance tolerances were retained. The 67 noncandidate cases in this particular filtered run were not executed.
- [First run](geometry-first.json) had four failures, including an outdated count expectation (14, not the three-animal Orchard's 8). The provisional east path swept across the tip of Spring Steps on a reverse departure; Burr's outside foot corners also overhung several original landing widths. [Original diagnostics](geometry-first.txt). Moving his seat inward supported the feet but the [second run](geometry-second.json) found six transfer interference failures. The final solution retains the original seat positions, widens the candidate boards and uses the corrected approach.
- [Native comparison run](world/native-first.json): **2/2 passed**. Compared renderer water height, water shader, shoreline water and real island tops against the current-state tide at turns 0/5/9, reaching both changes by actual UI actions. Cider Quay was submerged at tide 1 and stayed submerged; the two occupied shore groups started on dry support. At tide 3, Spring Steps also submerged after the passengers had moved. [Portrait observations](world/390x844/surfaces.json), [landscape](world/780x320/surfaces.json). The first attempted browser invocation failed to import JSON without an ESM import attribute ([original](world/results.json)); the data file is now read by the test harness and the bounded rerun passed.
- Run from the repo root with Node 24: `npm exec -- vitest run tests/unit/wide-ferry.test.ts tests/unit/wide-clearance.test.ts -t "Drowned Orchard"`. For native comparisons: `node node_modules/vite/bin/vite.js --config reviews/animal-presentation/recording-vite.config.ts --port 5366 --strictPort`, then `node node_modules/@playwright/test/cli.js test -c reviews/cove-nine-practice/world.config.ts`. The actual-play evidence records the independent minified build identity.
- These samples do not establish physical Android readability or GPU frame rate. The support tests cover the modeled bounds and sampled journeys, not every floating-point instant or an acoustic/accessibility evaluation.
