# Shared figurine checkpoint

[Matched model views](matched-models.png) compare the reviewed factory with the revised factory. Each column uses identical orthographic bounds, lights, scale, scene origin and viewing direction for both rows. The world is not rendered here. [Native profile samples](native-profiles.png) place the ten actual PNGs on two teal surfaces at 58px and 100px. Use the gameplay frames in the [main review](../REVIEW.md) to judge actual card size, composition and motion.

The pale rabbits have a joined lower muzzle, nose and lower lip, shaped eyelids and forepaws. The tan ear splay is asymmetric; the painted inset is attached to the vertices of each tapered ear. The sheep has a soft, continuous fleece, paired shoulder/haunch locks and a three-part forelock surrounding its darker face. The real four supported legs meet the fleece. The beaver sits with forepaws around its bib, visible paired incisors and a broad incised paddle resting against its flank. The deer keeps its marked chestnut flank, paired short forks, joined muzzle and four hooves. Parts use an animal-only matte material and smooth curved normals; shared ferry/world helpers and lighting did not change.

## Two derived views

Run `node tools/bake-animal-profiles.mjs` from the repository root with Node 24, project dependencies and the installed Playwright headless Chromium. The generator defaults to the project's `.cache/ms-playwright`; a configured `PLAYWRIGHT_BROWSERS_PATH` can identify an alternate installation. It reads the old factory from the reviewed Git object for comparators. No remote service or runtime renderer is required for a tile.

The generator instantiates the same production models and applies their true nonuniform world scales, then serializes **explicit vertices, normals, vertex paint, material and transforms** into an isolated offline Three.js renderer. It does not reconstruct the originals from primitive parameters. A hemisphere, warm directional key, cool fill and rim light gently describe the forms. Directional shadows, ACES output, a transparent 768px render and a high-quality downsample produce the 256px RGBA derivatives. Exact source digests, versions, camera/light settings, per-appearance scale/variant, painted pixel bounds, PNG byte counts and SHA-256s are in [the production manifest](../../../assets/portraits/animal-profiles.json).

Choice views show a head and upper chest. The high rabbit camera foreshortens upright ears while keeping their tips; the sheep head separates from its cream collar, and the beaver teeth and deer antlers stay inside the crop. Low sheep legs and the beaver paddle are omitted in the bust only to keep stray extremities off the lower edge. Nothing is stretched. Arrival uses the full original geometry and two opposing rabbit views. Every figure's measured alpha baseline is at native pixel 241; names remain in the UI. In the actual-size sheet, the sheep reads broader and the rabbits slimmer, with the deer standing tallest. This preserves the four species' different proportions.

## Corrections during comparison

- An initial exporter attempted to deserialize a parameterized legacy geometry unsupported by Three's core object loader. That run aborted and was not selected. The generator now transmits explicit buffers for every shape, including the editable curved normals and color data.
- The first complete studio sheet made the choice row look nearly identical to the standing row: feet appeared in several busts. The serialized root transform was stale after setting world scale. Updating its world matrices, then adjusting the five actual camera fields and checking the lower edge, produced the two distinct profiles.
- The first geometry batch passed 87/109. Its nonempty foot-set assertions detected excessive sole height and conservative rotated-ear boxes broke tight wide-ferry clearance. The sole now flattens smoothly toward the ground; oriented ear contours are baked into the model's local vertices. This keeps their rendered shapes and removes empty rotated bounding-box corners. No checks or boat/seat tolerances changed. [Original](../geometry-first.json), [109/109 intermediate](../geometry-second.json).
- Improving the visible paddle then exposed one conservative foot-plane envelope below -0.01: the initial rotated paddle box reached -0.023 despite the supported feet. Raising only its attachment by 0.017 units restored clearance, without changing seats or scaling the animal. [Original 108/109](../geometry-final.json), [final 109/109](../geometry-accepted.json).
- The sheep head is a single direct named group at the expected positive-Z head pivot. All original assertions passed. An additional measurement inside the existing Moss story test confirmed the head tips over 0.15 radians, all eight ear/eye/nose/forelock centers move over 0.002 local units, the four independent feet stay within 1e-8 and every measured facial center restores within 1e-8. [All 14 story-staging cases passed again](nod-hierarchy.json); these repeat a subset of the 109 rather than increasing that count. Deck, transfer, support and seating thresholds remain unchanged.

[Per-model budget and exact matched camera values](model-costs-and-cameras.json) report precise local vertex bounds and nominal color-pass draw calls per piece. This is separate from full-scene draw costs including shadows. No physical-device performance or human visual approval is claimed.
