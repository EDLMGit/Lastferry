`recording-results.json` is an additional passing bounded actual-UI capture, not another distinct functional case. Its first eleven actions were played through the real UI with Reduced Motion; Settings restored Follow device/normal before recording. The uninterrupted 14.546-second selected clip shows the final two passages, final unloading, visible arrival, Settings/Back, dismissal, settled Harbor and reopening. It does not claim to be a complete rescue recording. `MEDIA.json` records all 98 delivered native frames, timestamps, codec, runtime audio provenance and the 0.667-second largest capture gap. No audio gain, frame interpolation, cuts or gameplay timing changes were applied.

Final selected real-game frames are `gameplay/390x844-three.png`, `gameplay/780x320-three.png`, `gameplay/390x844-details.png` and `gameplay/390x844-closed-harbor.png`. Matched old actual rescues are under `before/`. The six-neighbor and failure-image images under `fixtures/` are deliberately labeled synthetic renderer specimens. `EVIDENCE.json` records each selected file's exact hash, native dimensions and source qualification.

Historical `cove-eight-practice` and `three-lanterns` browser helpers/assertions were updated for scored Ternhaven, Details disclosure and the current accessible result label. Those entire older suites were not rerun; the focused cases above exercise their affected result/acceptance contracts. Generic unscored behavior remains covered by the dedicated unit fixture.

Reproduction uses Node 24 and the repository's Playwright Chromium, one worker, DPR 1, SwiftShader, touch/mobile emulation. `ENVIRONMENT.json` contains exact commands and temporary config contents. The development server was restarted after CSS edits because no-watch Vite preserves transformed modules. The final served CSS is retained in `served-arrival-css.txt`. `build-final.txt`, `typecheck-final.txt` and `assets-final.txt` retain final check output. The production build retains the existing >500kB bundle warning.

Native screenshots and representative raw moving frames were inspected at native size. Names, lantern state, primary/Replay/Undo and Settings remain readable. The open portrait card covers most of the Harbor; sea margins and a small dock edge remain. Closing it reveals the coherent centered Harbor and full lighthouse rather than moving the world aside. Expanded Details intentionally replaces the portrait area with bounded scrolling text; controls remain outside it, including the short-landscape storage-notice case.

Physical Android/iOS chrome, spoken screen-reader output, device haptics and human listening acceptance were not established here. Fullscreen entered in the tested Chromium surface and included the common app/dialog root; that is not a physical phone promise. Background recovery was exercised with a synthetic visibility event. The score/audio runtime is preserved, and actual output was recorded, but this pass adds no fresh physical-speaker or human listening validation. No deployment was performed.
