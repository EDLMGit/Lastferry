## Review findings behind the correction

**Wide was left unchanged.** The b645→5085 diff adds Reef Return but does not change `data/wide-berth.level.json`. Fresh execution of the exact production rules confirms all six passengers can be staged at High Quay by action 9, followed by 100 waits and 20 Harbor–High Quay round trips, then a win at action 156. High Quay stays dry and the settled homeward network remains open. A safe landing is legitimate; the authored puzzle needs a meaningful return decision.

**The current mechanic is now implemented.** Direction is authored per tide phase, moves validate current direction and depth, and scheduled changes follow the committed action. Definite return cutoff can explain an irrecoverable tide error. Use this work in Wide rather than building another system or map. Cutoff detection is directed reachability, not proof that every surviving state has a complete capacity/deadline solution.

**Reef's two witnesses are a small variation.** Both follow the same hub route, stage Heather at Cairn Quay, retrieve the pool group and cross home fully loaded on action 8. The difference is whether Flick stays aboard or waits with Heather. Independent enumeration through safe full-group Harbor arrival found those two histories; harmless waits and delivery permutations afterward were excluded. This structural result does not alone measure difficulty, but does not override the user's unsatisfied playtest.

**The large boat has a proportion problem.** Its declared beam grows from 1.06 to 2.4 and bow-plus-stern extent from 1.93 to 4.21, while capacity changes from four to six and the rendered hull layers keep their depths. Native frames show a broad flat deck with a cabin at one end. Broad T dock heads and right-angle gangways visually merge into that platform. Repair the common boat/landing assets for both levels; keep animals visible and the shoreline, dock, bridge and boat visually distinct.

**Same-ID repair does not inherently require a migration.** Current saves are cove-boundary checkpoints, not mid-puzzle snapshots. Preserve chapter IDs/order, scene boundaries and completed history. Rebalance Wide under `wide-berth`; do not interpret save preservation as a ban on changing its puzzle. Full mid-puzzle restoration remains an unresolved future requirement.
