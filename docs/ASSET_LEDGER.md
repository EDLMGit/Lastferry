# M1 production assets

Runtime geometry is original code authored for this project by Codex, starting on 2026-10-20; later additions are dated below. No third-party models, images, fonts, audio recordings, or generated bitmap assets are loaded by the game. Project code remains subject to the project's chosen rights; this ledger does not assert a new distribution license.

The supplied images in `references/` remain generated concept references only. `first-crossing.png` was inspected for direction; it is never loaded by the runtime. Gameplay evidence lives separately under `reviews/m1/`.

M1.1 refines the same original animal meshes: taller silhouettes, wider separated rabbit ears, cream/tan coats and a darker sheep face. Named passenger anchors under the ferry's animated hull now carry aboard animals through draft, bob, tide and travel. The phone camera fits actual scene bounds without changing authored gameplay coordinates. No acquired or generated bitmap assets were added. Fresh refinement evidence is under `reviews/m1-1/`.

Holding Pier experiment (2026-10-21): reuses these same procedural production meshes and generated sound cues. Three islands and piers come from `data/holding-pier.level.json`; the safe holding pier uses grey stone boards and the rabbit shoreline is lowered to elevation 1. The shallow-water shader now derives its highlighted corridor from authored shallow edges instead of fixed First Crossing coordinates. No downloaded assets, concept-image backgrounds or new licensed materials were added.

Bellwater Cove (2026-10-22): `data/bellwater.level.json` authors a triangular inlet and its six passages. `src/scene/layout.ts` owns the necessary scenery anchors, pier materials, camera and modest local lighting choices, retaining the older coves' settings. `src/scene/scenery.ts` extracts the unchanged cottage and adds original code-built reed clumps and a bronze bell under a timber frame. Low Pier has sandy reed edges; Bell Pier has a rocky pickup shore. Tansy, Lark and Bram reuse the existing rabbit/sheep meshes and deck anchors through species ordering. No external or generated bitmap asset was added. Shared water remains the existing shader; the broader water/shore treatment is deferred to the next milestone. Fresh gameplay evidence is under `reviews/bellwater/`.
