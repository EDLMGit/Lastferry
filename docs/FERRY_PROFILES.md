# Authored ferry presentation profiles

The active level alone resolves `ferryProfile(level)`: capacity four retains the original factory, anchors, movement blending, transfer gates and wake recipe. Capacity six selects the actual wider yellow hull/red cabin in `models.ts`. There is no fleet, unlocked global hull or packing interaction. `ferry()` without arguments remains the exact original model; geometry attributes, indices, transforms and colors match reviewed `7e324e8` byte-for-byte (`reviews/wide-berth/original-model-audit.json`).

Production placement lives in `src/scene/ferry-profile.ts`. `deckSeat` gives fixed identity-sorted seats for the new roster: two full-size sheep across the aft open deck and four rabbits in two forward rows, leaving a central aisle. Seats never depend on selected membership, so nobody staying aboard snaps when another passenger transfers. Existing coves retain their original species-ordinal anchors and unchanged scales. Six new neighbors can stand in a stable horizontal identity row on the broad pier; individual slots do not collapse after partial transfers.

The six-space hull's actual modeled XZ bounds are approximately ±1.471 and −1.850 to 3.034. Wide Berth's larger pier heads and berth spacing were authored for the real model. `createPier` is the same shared factory used by World and the physical route tests. `wideTransferPath` traverses a clear shore front aisle, the visible wood gangway, the open bow gate, central deck aisle and destination seat. A selected group is deterministically sequenced within its existing accepted action duration, with one transaction and the existing grouped wooden contact. Winning groups finish their visible disembarkation by the unchanged arrival-cue boundary at 72% of the 900ms completion, then settle; ordinary action durations and audio timing remain unchanged. Everybody not yet moving stays attached to their live deck/shore pose; completed boarders follow the current draft transform. Original species/cove choreography is unchanged.

`hullDraftY(requiredDraft)` is the shared visual displacement formula, with deck feet still 0.065 above water at draft four before bobbing. Gameplay uses the real reducer's integer draft and tide. New wake contact uses the same authored hull outline, outward bevel margin and a wide-only birth span; actual rendered hull projection checks cover 32 directions. Existing four-space ellipse, birth width, palette, spacing, lifetime, independent released events and finite docking fade remain intact.

The developer gallery `#wide-ferry` uses the actual factory plus production seats/scales for one sheep and all four rabbits (five bodies/six spaces); it is a static design specimen, not gameplay evidence. Focused tests enumerate every capacity-legal authored roster and boarding/unloading selection at all four actual piers, both headings and each valid dry tide, with conservative bounds derived from the actual animal meshes against one another, cabin and rails. They use production draft, profile, settling and placement helpers. Route tests sweep actual occupied ferry/pier meshes separately. Native normal-speed phone inspection remains necessary to judge the quick group choreography, contact, full-load readability and scene fit; numerical checks do not establish human art, acoustic or device approval.
