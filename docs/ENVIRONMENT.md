## Visual and motion contract

One cached course per edge must serve the visible dots, selected/blocked emphasis, boat position, tangent, shallow-water treatment and clearance verification. Exact stopping endpoints are preserved. Interior bends are rounded locally, traversed by approximate arc length in either direction, with the existing brief acceleration and deceleration. The boat clears its pier broadside before steering and returns to its stable endpoint pose. It never continues automatically through a stop.

Broad blue/teal variation and sparse irregular quiet highlights replace the repeated fine diagonal pattern. Shallow banks belong to the inlet rather than a luminous route stripe. Water color remains presentation only; integer depths and the reducer determine legal travel.

Exposed, wet and submerged shore treatments follow the actual rendered water height during transitions and settle to the committed tide. Flooded piers stay flooded at their exact rule threshold. Remaining reeds and rocks sit over visible submerged ground. Contact accents stay secondary and avoid dotted navigation-like outlines. Free forecast shows the actual next tide without altering committed state.

Shore refinements remain within the existing validated land envelope. Selected silhouettes and grouped reeds replace regular detail rings; the miniature's overall compositions, fixed camera and passenger prominence remain. Warm matte light and soft contact shadow support the ferry and animals.

A short fading wake follows actual ferry travel and stops emitting at arrival. A restrained warm Harbor response acknowledges successful unloading. Undo, Restart, level changes and cancelled transitions reset presentation effects. Reduced motion disables moving decorative effects while keeping state feedback and input protection intact.

## Verification boundary

Unit checks cover production curved geometry, exact endpoints, reverse travel, finite/degenerate samples, distance progression, continuous steering, actual full-ferry rotated clearance and network crossings/contacts. Old polylines or boat-center-only checks cannot establish the changed motion's safety. The existing reducer witnesses, losses, subsets, conservation and complete Undo remain required.

Actual UI checks cover all three coves, loaded travel near piers and around outer bends in both directions, both tide rises and restoration, arrival/reset, free inspection, natural keyboard and emulated touch, rapid Confirm protection, phone layouts and the intermediate-height responsive regression. Production play must work without development controls or external assets.

Matched screenshots and a short uninterrupted actual-play recording assess visual and motion quality. Before/after draw calls, triangle counts and frame intervals use the same browser, viewport and measurement protocol. Software-rendered automation does not establish physical-phone frame rates.

Implementation details, actual results, source identities, evidence and remaining limitations are recorded in `reviews/environment/REVIEW.md`. Historical Bellwater evidence remains under its original source; a changed shared renderer receives fresh browser coverage.
