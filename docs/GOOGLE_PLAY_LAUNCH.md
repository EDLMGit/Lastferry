# Last Ferry · Google Play launch plan

The twelve-cove Journey ends at the lighthouse table. In v0.1.5 the Harbor log offers earned revisits, including First Crossing for captains who already have its lantern record. Nine coves award lanterns. Keep Drowned Orchard, Lantern Reach, Far Shore and Last Ferry unscored for this playtest. The release decision on those four belongs to game direction after observing players. Every player who brings the final group home can reach supper and credits.

## 1. Put the same voyage on a phone

The owner’s v0.1.4 playtest exposed a forced visit to Home after Moss’s reward. The v0.1.5 playtest gives each new keepsake an illustrated arrival and continues directly to the saved story. The release-bundle rehearsal follows the owner’s playtest of this handoff. [Journey rewards](../reviews/journey-rewards/REVIEW.md)

On a physical phone, launch in airplane mode and let a newcomer choose Play. At Holding Pier ask, **“What are you trying to do, and what do you expect to change first?”** Record what they point at before explaining the tide sign. Watch a rescue and Undo. Check the crowded Wide Berth, the moving bridge, rotation, Android Back, interruptions, speaker audio and a return to an earned supper. Try TalkBack on the passenger selection and arrival actions. If the player misses a cue, keep the observation and have Codex fix the smallest cause before the next test.

## 2. Set up the publisher in parallel

The owner chooses the account type, completes verification and keeps control of Play Console, recovery access and the release credentials. New registration costs US$25 once. New personal accounts also have a real Android-device verification step. Check the account's registration date and the production-access requirements shown on its dashboard. [Google Play account setup](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435) · [Device verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14316361)

Choose the publisher name, support contact, permanent application ID, launch countries and free or upfront-paid pricing. A listing offered free cannot later become a paid download under that package name. Test pricing and audience decisions before first publication. My recommendation for version one is offline play with no ads or analytics. The owner decides the commercial model. [Google Play pricing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6334373)

## 3. Install the Play-delivered version

First, Codex installs a release-mode rehearsal bundle locally using a temporary application ID and verifies an in-place update. Once the owner approves the permanent application ID, Codex prepares a versioned release Android App Bundle (AAB) for the internal Play track. The current minimum target for a new ordinary phone game is Android 16 / API 36. The minimum supported device version is a separate compatibility choice. The owner enrolls in Play App Signing and protects the upload key and its backup outside source control. Upload the signed bundle to internal testing. Install from Google Play, open offline, earn a checkpoint and test an upgrade through the same track. Use the pre-launch report to identify devices that need hands-on investigation. [Target API levels](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878) · [Android App Bundles](https://developer.android.com/guide/app-bundle/) · [Play App Signing](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish/app-signing) · [Pre-launch reports](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9844487)

## 4. Test with people and prepare the listing

For a personal developer account created after 13 November 2023, Google requires at least 12 closed-track testers to stay opted in continuously for at least 14 days before the production-access application. Recruit a few extra for dropouts. Use the Play opt-in link, record feedback from people who actually play, note which issues were fixed, and check the dashboard. The clock starts with qualified closed-track opt-ins. A privately shared APK or internal test is a separate activity. For another account type or an older account, follow its dashboard and still run a focused beta. Allow time after testing for the production-access decision and the app review. [Production access for new personal accounts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465) · [Testing tracks](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334)

Game direction owns the listing draft and screenshots. Describe the game as a turn-based ferry puzzle. Show the tide gauge with the waiting passengers at Holding Pier, a choice at the swing bridge, and a lit-lantern arrival. Save the supper scene for players to discover. Prepare the original 512 × 512 app icon, 1024 × 500 feature graphic, phone screenshots, descriptions and support contact. Check distribution rights and required credits for the shipped art, audio and dependencies. [Preview assets](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151)

The owner confirms the intended age groups, content rating questionnaire, ads and app-access answers, and the Data safety declaration against the shipped app and every bundled SDK. Publish an accessible privacy policy and make it available in the app and on the store listing. If children are part of the intended audience, review Families requirements against the final bundle. [Prepare the app for review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455) · [Data safety](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469) · [User data and privacy policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311)

## 5. Make the release decision

Game direction reviews the fresh-player observation, real handset performance, the store-installed update and how the four late coves feel. Codex closes reproducible blockers and delivers the signed release candidate. The owner confirms the listing, price and launch countries, applies for production access where required, and authorizes production publication after Google review. Watch Android vitals for crashes, hangs and slow sessions, alongside player reports. Keep the tested bundle, version code and signed build procedure for the first patch. [Prepare a release](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859348) · [Android vitals](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16409982)

**Timing.** Android packaging, account setup and tester recruitment can start together. For an account subject to the closed-test rule, budget several weeks from a working Android candidate. Fourteen continuous days is the testing floor. Device fixes and Google's decisions determine the actual launch date.
